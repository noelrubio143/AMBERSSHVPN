AMBERSSH VPN ("the app", "we", "us") is a tunneling / VPN app for Android and Fire OS. This policy explains what information the app and its related services collect, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

3. VPN / tunnel function

The app uses Android's VpnService only to create the tunnel you start by tapping Connect. It sets up traffic routing through the server or configuration you choose so that your device's traffic is sent through that tunnel. The app does not use this to read, modify or sell the content of your traffic. The server or network operator you connect to may have its own policies, so please only use servers you trust.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free. It shows ads from Google AdMob: banner, interstitial, app-open and rewarded ads. Free VPN time is earned by watching a rewarded ad (2 hours per ad, up to a stored maximum of 2 hours). The app has no subscriptions, purchases or payments.

AdMob may use the Advertising ID and similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA and the UK), the app shows a Google consent form before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later from Account → Privacy & ad settings (press and hold the free-time text on the home screen) when this option is shown.

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID or turn off personalization in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK - advertising.
  • Google User Messaging Platform - consent forms.
  • Firebase Realtime Database and Cloud Messaging - online counter and notifications (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Presence entry: removed automatically when the app disconnects.
  • Device ID, free-time balance, settings and your configs: kept on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data: handled by Google under its own retention policies.

To request access to or deletion of data linked to your device ID, e-mail us at noelrubios1201@gmail.com with your device ID (press and hold the free-time text in the app and choose "Copy Device ID"). We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Traffic sent through the app's tunnel is protected by the protocol of the configuration you choose (for example SSH, V2Ray/Xray or OpenVPN). Connections to Google and Firebase services use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are requested for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access or request deletion of data linked to your device ID (section 6).
  • Withdraw or change ad consent in the app's privacy options, or reset your Advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Stop all data collection by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or Philippines), you may have additional rights under local data protection law. Contact us to exercise them.
