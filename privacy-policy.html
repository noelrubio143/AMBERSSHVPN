1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AMBERSSH VPN ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use our mobile application, available on the Amazon Appstore and other distribution platforms.

By downloading, installing, or using AMBERSSH VPN, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account or configuration details you enter (e.g., server/config selections, saved settings)
  • Support messages you send to us, including your email address

Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and unique device/advertising identifiers
  • App usage information: features used, session duration, crash reports, and general performance diagnostics
  • Approximate network information required to establish a VPN connection (e.g., selected server region)

3. VPN Usage & Logging

AMBERSSH VPN is designed to route your internet traffic through our servers to help protect your connection. We want to be transparent about what this involves:

  • We do not sell your browsing history or the content of your traffic to third parties.
  • Connection metadata (such as connection timestamps, session duration, bandwidth used, and selected server) may be temporarily logged for troubleshooting, abuse prevention, and service quality purposes.
  • We do not intentionally monitor, inspect, or store the content of your web traffic (e.g., websites visited, files transferred) beyond what is technically necessary to route the connection in real time.
